Survey Mission

Status: Ok.

Defines a survey polygon and generates a lawnmower coverage path inside it, published as a nav_msgs/Path or geometry_msgs/PoseArray.

Distance between adjacent grid lines across the marked area.

The angle of the grid lines, relative to East/ENU.

Amount of additional distance to add outside the survey area for vehicle turning.

By default, transits between survey lines follow the zone boundary: the area outside the polygon can't be assumed clear, and cutting across the survey interior is avoided. Enabling this connects transits directly instead, shown as light gray links.

X/Y Axis: Edit the polygon.

  • Add Vertices: Click on the view area.
  • Delete Vertices: Tap an existing vertex.
  • Move Vertices: Drag an existing vertex to reposition it.
  • Start (ST) and Finish (FIN) markers appear once 3 vertices exist. They can be dragged but not deleted.
  • Snap to Grid: Hold the Shift key while dragging to snap to the pixel grid.

Z Axis: Edit polygon vertex heights.

  • Set Height: Click and drag a vertex to set a rough Z value.
  • Fine Adjust: Release and drag a vertex a second time to fine adjust its height.

When editing X/Y/Z positions, click the icon again to stop editing. Waypoint heights are interpolated from the polygon edge heights, editable in Z mode.
